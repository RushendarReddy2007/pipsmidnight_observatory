A Night in the Life of Pip

Scroll through Pip’s nocturnal shift from the stroke of midnight to the sleepy pink dawn.

1
00:00 · Stroke of Midnight
🕰️

The Midnight Wakeup

The brass nursery clock chimes twelve times. Pip wriggles awake inside a nest of cloud pillows, rubs drowsy eye sockets until they glow warm amber, and gently adjusts the starry nightcap bow. Another shift of dream engineering has begun!

2
01:15 · The Starlit Garden
✨

Gathering Dream Ingredients

Carrying a tiny woven basket, Pip floats among the constellations to harvest sleepy ingredients: glowing stardust, drifting lavender breeze, and three soft sighs from the drowsy moon. Every dream needs the freshest starlight to keep nightmares away.

3
02:30 · The Lullaby Lab
🎼

Composing at the Lab

Back in the workshop, Pip turns the golden key of the music box. As the gears hum, Pip pours in the stardust. The notes melt together into a soft, calming melody that smells like warm milk and fresh gingerbread.

4
03:45 · The Bedside Glow
🏮

Lighting the Lanterns

Before setting out, Pip checks every nightlight. With a gentle tap, Pip awakens the amber flame inside the porcelain crescent moon lamps, casting peaceful shadows across every dark room so children feel safe and cherished.

5
04:50 · Over the Rooftops
🚀

Delivering Across the Skies

Steering a fluffy cloud through the cool evening mist, Pip hovers over slumbering towns. One by one, dream bubbles are dropped down chimneys: dreams of flying on friendly dragons, swimming in chocolate lakes, and winning big hackathons!

6
06:00 · The Pink Dawn
🌅

Snuggling Into Slumber

The horizon warms into blush pink and buttery yellow. Pip lets out a long baby yawn that rattles the star chimes. Back at the nursery, Pip snuggles under a cloud blanket—ready to fall asleep inside the very dreams Pip just engineered.
